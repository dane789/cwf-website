Website review and next steps

Board review checklist

Use this page to confirm the public information, identify what is still needed, and agree on the next updates to the Crusader Wrestling Foundation website.

Visibility: This page is not linked from the public website and search engines are asked not to index it. It is not password-protected. Anyone with the URL can view or share it, so do not add EINs, private contact information, or confidential board material.
Open the public website
01

Confirm the public identity and mission

The public site uses the name Crusader Wrestling Foundation and describes it as an independent Pennsylvania nonprofit corporation.

  • Confirm the public name and organization description match the approved records.
  • Review the phrase “our wrestling program” in the mission and decide whether it clearly describes the foundation’s independent role.
  • Approve any wording change before it is published.
Board review needed
02

Complete the Gold Out fundraiser information

The site currently lists pre-orders through November 30 at $20 and an event-day price of $25.

  • Confirm the prices, order deadline, available sizes, and event date.
  • Provide the approved order form URL and payment instructions.
  • Confirm pickup time and location, then update the page when details are final.
  • Decide when the fundraiser notice should be removed after the order period.
Details needed
03

Approve sponsor and donation instructions

  • Choose a public contact email or other contact method for sponsors and families.
  • Approve donation instructions or the fundraising platform the foundation will use.
  • Decide whether the foundation will publish sponsorship levels or recognition benefits.
  • Keep tax-deductibility statements accurate. The website currently says the IRS determination is pending.
Board decision needed
04

Verify leadership and public documents

  • Confirm the officer names and titles shown on the public website.
  • Decide whether to publish approved governing documents or an annual summary.
  • Review any document for personal addresses, signatures, financial account details, or other private information before sharing.
Board review needed
05

Choose the next public updates

  • Confirm the service area and public mailing address, if either should appear on the site.
  • Choose a domain when the foundation is ready to move beyond temporary GitHub Pages hosting.
  • Share approved photos and short program updates as activities happen. Obtain permission before publishing identifiable student photos.
Plan when ready

After board review

Record decisions through the board’s normal process. Once the public wording and details are approved, they can be added to the main website. This page does not collect or save board responses.
