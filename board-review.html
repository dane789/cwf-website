Mission and services

A clear direction for CWF

The approved governance packet describes CWF as an independent charitable organization that can support wrestling programs and student-athletes through equipment, training, competition expenses, nutrition, educational opportunities, and youth wrestling activities. It also emphasizes sportsmanship, discipline, leadership, character, academic achievement, and amateur athletics.

What the current website communicates

The site describes support for equipment, training, travel, meals, lodging, team activities, and volunteers. These are consistent with the support role in the bylaws. The public mission phrase “our wrestling program” could sound like CWF operates or belongs to one team, which may not convey the independent role clearly.

Possible wording for board discussion

Draft only. This is not adopted or published mission language.

Crusader Wrestling Foundation supports student-athletes and wrestling programs by helping provide equipment, training, competition opportunities, nutrition, and educational resources. We seek to help athletes build sportsmanship, discipline, character, confidence, and leadership for success on the mat and in life.

Board members can decide whether this better describes the foundation’s independent support role and whether to retain “Faith · Opportunity · Leadership” as the public tagline.

What similar organizations make clear

Beat the Streets Philadelphia and New York describe who their programs serve, what participants receive, and how wrestling connects to personal development. Wrestle Like A Girl names its audience and leadership outcomes. Pennsylvania Junior Wrestling emphasizes a safe learning environment, wrestling skills, respect, and sportsmanship.

What CWF can take from that

Use a simple service promise and report results when they can be measured. CWF does not need to claim it runs mentoring, academic, or year-round club programs unless the board approves a plan, budget, delivery partner, eligibility rules, and safeguards for those services.

Suggested service scope

  • Current support areas: equipment and training, competition and travel, meals and lodging, youth wrestling activities, and athlete development opportunities approved by the board.
  • Potential future programs: mentoring, academic help, leadership workshops, or broader youth programming only after the board defines who is served, who delivers the service, the budget, safety practices, and how results will be reviewed.
  • Events: Senior Night and a banquet can support athlete recognition and community, subject to an approved charitable purpose and budget.

Reference organizations

  • Beat the Streets Philadelphia, youth wrestling programs and development.
  • Beat the Streets New York Junior League, structured wrestling and life learning.
  • Wrestle Like A Girl, leadership and wrestling programs for girls and women.
  • Pennsylvania Junior Wrestling, youth wrestling mission and values.
