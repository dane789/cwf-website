Our purpose

Supporting wrestlers.
Building futures.

Our mission is to support our wrestling program and student-athletes by helping create opportunities to develop skills, character, confidence, and leadership both on and off the mat.

Preparing student-athletes for success in wrestling and, more importantly, for life beyond the sport.

CWF is an independent nonprofit organization that brings parents, guardians, alumni, and community supporters together to support wrestling student-athletes.
